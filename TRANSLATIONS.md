# Translation Notes

Curated notes for non-obvious translation choices in `dict/en.json` and `dict/fr.json`. Add an entry whenever a translation is non-trivial or potentially debatable.

## Conventions

- `NAV` (Hungarian tax authority) — kept as `NAV` in both EN and FR. It's a proper noun.
- `ÁFA` → EN `VAT`, FR `TVA`. Standard fiscal terms.
- `ügyfél` / `partner` → EN `customer` / `partner`, FR `client` / `partenaire`.
- `bizonylat` → EN `document`, FR `document`. Generic Hungarian fiscal term covering invoices, receipts, delivery notes, etc.
- `bizonylattömb` → EN `document block` / `invoice block`, FR `bloc de bizonylats` ... TBD per context.

## Decisions log

- 2026-04-29 — `Díjbekérő` → EN `Proforma invoice`, FR `Facture proforma`. Hungarian `díjbekérő` is literally a "fee request" — closest international equivalent is a proforma invoice. `Payment request` was considered but is too generic and would clash with `Fizetési kérelem`.
- 2026-04-29 — `Stornó` / `Stornózás` → EN `Cancellation` / `Cancel document`, FR `Annulation` / `Annuler le document`. The word "storno" is Italian/German commercial vocabulary embedded in Hungarian accounting law. Kept the concept but used natural-language equivalents; avoided the loanword "storno" in EN/FR as it would be opaque.
- 2026-04-29 — `Bizonylattömb` → EN `Invoice block`, FR `Bloc de documents`. A Hungarian fiscal concept: a numbered pre-printed book of document forms. `Invoice block` is a compromise — `document block` is more accurate but less recognisable in a billing UI context. For standalone `Bizonylattömb` entries used `Invoice block`; for `Bizonylattömbök` (plural) and contexts mentioning generic document types, used `Bloc de documents`.
- 2026-04-29 — `Teljesítés` → EN `Performance / Delivery`, FR `Exécution / Livraison`. In Hungarian fiscal law, `teljesítés` is the "date of supply/performance" on an invoice (a VAT concept). No single English word covers both service delivery and goods delivery equally well; the slash form was chosen so the user understands the scope. In narrower contexts (`Teljesítés dátuma`) `Completion date` was used.
- 2026-04-29 — `Módosító számla` → EN `Corrective invoice`, FR `Facture rectificative`. Hungarian law uses `módosító számla` (literally "modifying invoice") for what EU VAT Directive calls a "credit note" or "corrective invoice". Chose `Corrective invoice` / `Facture rectificative` over `Credit note` / `Avoir` because the HU concept can represent both upward and downward adjustments.
- 2026-04-29 — `Visszavonás` → EN `Cancel / Revoke`, FR `Annuler / Révoquer`. The verb is used in two distinct UI contexts: revoking an API key or invitation (→ Revoke) and cancelling an action (→ Cancel). Both glosses given with slash notation since the key appears in different widgets.
- 2026-04-29 — `Megjegyzés` → EN `Note`, FR `Remarque`. `Megjegyzés` can mean "comment", "remark" or "note" depending on context. `Note` chosen for EN (shorter, standard invoice vocabulary); `Remarque` for FR rather than `Note` or `Commentaire` because it is the standard neutral term in French professional documents.
- 2026-04-29 — `Megnevezés` → EN `Name / Description`, FR `Libellé`. In invoice line items `megnevezés` means the line-item description/label. `Libellé` (account/item label) is the correct French accounting term; English uses a slash to cover both field usages.
- 2026-04-29 — `Mégse`, `Partnerek`, `Partner`, `Nyugta`, `Műveletek` retained despite absence from the 4593-entry catalog snapshot. These are core UI chrome (Cancel button, Partners menu, Receipt, Actions menu) explicitly required by the acceptance criteria; their absence from the catalog is likely a scraping gap rather than an indication they don't exist in the product.
- 2026-04-29 — `Kiadás` → EN `Expense`, FR `Dépense`. Could also be `Expenditure` (EN) or `Charge` (FR) in an accounting context, but `Expense` / `Dépense` are the most natural terms for a day-to-day invoicing/bookkeeping UI used by small businesses.
- 2026-04-29 (batch 2) — `Előlegszámla` → EN `Advance invoice`, FR `Facture d'acompte`. Hungarian `előleg` = "advance / deposit". `Advance invoice` is the standard English billing term; `Facture d'acompte` is the standard French equivalent (preferred over `Facture d'avance`).
- 2026-04-29 (batch 2) — `Fordított adózás` / `F.AFA (Fordított ÁFA)` → EN `Reverse charge` / `Reverse charge VAT`, FR `Autoliquidation de TVA` / `TVA en autoliquidation`. EU-standard VAT mechanism terminology. `Fordított` = reversed/inverted. The abbreviated label `F.AFA` retained to match what the UI displays.
- 2026-04-29 (batch 2) — `K.AFA (Különbözeti ÁFA)` → EN `Margin scheme VAT`, FR `TVA sur marge`. `Különbözeti` = "differential/margin". EU VAT Directive Art. 311 margin scheme terminology applied.
- 2026-04-29 (batch 2) — `Bevételezés` / `Kivételezés` → EN `Goods receipt` / `Goods issue`, FR `Réception de marchandises` / `Sortie de stock`. Standard warehouse/logistics vocabulary. `Bevételezés` (literally "receipting") = goods coming into stock; `Kivételezés` (literally "issuing") = goods leaving stock. ISO warehouse terminology used.
- 2026-04-29 (batch 2) — `Szváziföld` → EN `Eswatini`, FR `Eswatini`. The kingdom renamed itself from Swaziland in 2018; modern standard name used.
- 2026-04-29 (batch 2) — `Működő` → EN `Active`, FR `Actif`. In the company-info context `működő` means the company is currently operating. Chose `Active` (same as `Aktív`) rather than `Operating` to keep UI badge vocabulary consistent; these appear in different filter contexts so collision is not a problem.
- 2026-04-29 (batch 2) — `Kintlévő` → EN `Outstanding`, FR `En attente de paiement`. `Kintlévő` = "outstanding receivable" (a debt still owed). EN `Outstanding` is the standard accounts-receivable term; FR `En attente de paiement` (awaiting payment) is more natural than the literal `En souffrance` in a UI context.
- 2026-05-24 (P1 delta — editor + promo capture) — `Pályázat` → EN `grant`, FR `appel à projets`. These are EU/innovation funding calls (`vissza nem térítendő támogatás` = non-repayable grant), so `grant` / `appel à projets` is more accurate than `tender`/`appel d'offres`. `Pályázatfigyelő` → EN `Grant Monitor`, FR `Veille des appels à projets`.
- 2026-05-24 — `Céginfó` / `Céginformáció` → EN `Company Info`, FR `Infos entreprise`. `Partnerfigyelő` / `Partnerfigyelés` → EN `Partner Monitor` / `Partner Monitoring`, FR `Veille des partenaires`. Consistent "Monitor/Veille" family with Grant Monitor and the existing tender-monitor naming.
- 2026-05-24 — `Folyamatos teljesítés` → EN `Continuous performance`, FR `Prestation continue`. The VAT "continuous supply" concept on invoices; follows the existing `Teljesítés` → Performance/Exécution convention.
- 2026-05-24 — `Adattörlő kód` → EN `Data erasure code`, FR `Code d'effacement des données`. Hungarian NAV receipt requirement for durable data carriers (Gov. Decree 726/2020).
- 2026-05-24 — `Technikai felhasználó` → EN `Technical user`, FR `Utilisateur technique`; `aláírókulcs` → `signing key` / `clé de signature`; `cserekulcs` → `exchange key` / `clé d'échange`. NAV Online Számla integration terms. `KAÜ` (Központi Azonosítási Ügynök) kept as proper noun, glossed `Central Authentication Agent` / `Agent d'authentification central`. `adózó` → `taxpayer` / `contribuable`; `Korm. rendelet` → `Government Decree` / `décret gouvernemental`.
- 2026-05-24 — `Közlemény` (bank transaction context) → EN `Payment reference`, FR `Communication`. The free-text note on a bank transfer, not an "announcement". FR `Communication` is the standard bank-transfer field label.
- 2026-05-24 — `ügymenet` / `Ügykezelő` → EN `case` / `case manager`, FR `dossier` / `gestionnaire de dossiers`. Billingo's "dossier" feature (`/n/document/dossier`) groups documents per partner; `case`/`dossier` fits the UI better than literal "course of business".
- 2026-05-24 — Billingo's own postal address (`1054 Budapest, Honvéd utca 8. 1. em. 2. ajtó`) kept verbatim (identity translation) in EN and FR — addresses are not localized. Captured because `Honvéd` carries a HU diacritic.
- 2026-05-24 — Price labels (`3.790 Ft + ÁFA / hó`, `… / év`) — number + `Ft` kept as displayed (incl. NBSP thousands separators); only `ÁFA`→VAT/TVA and `/ hó`→`/ mo`/`/ mois`, `/ év`→`/ yr`/`/ an` localized.
- 2026-05-24 (P1 interactive capture — editor dropdowns) — `ÁKK` kept verbatim (identity) in EN+FR. It is the acronym of the Államadósság Kezelő Központ (Hungarian Govt Debt Management Agency), used as an FX-rate source option alongside MNB; an institution acronym like NAV/MNB, not translated.
- 2026-05-24 — `Tétel közvetlen beszúrása` → EN `Insert item directly`, FR `Insérer une ligne directement`. Option in the invoice-editor item dropdown to add a free line vs. pick from the product catalog. `Tétel` = invoice line item.
- 2026-07-31 (pattern layer) — Keys holding a `:token` (`:type letöltése`, `Új :type`) or a number (`3 db`) are now compiled into runtime patterns, so they also translate the substituted text the app actually renders. Two consequences for translators:
  - **Keep the `:token` in the target string.** A translation that drops it (`Company registration number: :number …` → `""`) cannot be rebuilt from the captured text, and the pattern is discarded.
  - **The grammar of the source pair is reused.** `Új :type` → `Nouveau :type` renders `Nouvelle facture` as `Nouveau facture`, and `3 db` → `3 pièces` renders `1 pièces`. Patterns only fire when no exact key matched, so add the exact key when the agreement matters. For number keys, prefer writing the plural form — the compiler keeps the largest-numbered pair of a given shape for exactly this reason.
- 2026-05-24 — `Kezdd el beírni a keresett cég vagy partner nevét!` → EN `Start typing the name of the company or partner you're looking for!`, FR `Commence à saisir le nom de l'entreprise ou du partenaire recherché !`. Empty-state hint of the partner search combobox.
